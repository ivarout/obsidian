## Hiking

#### 1. Lac de Gaube & Vignemale (From Cauterets, France)

- **The Vibe:** Waterfalls, crystal-clear high-altitude lakes, and views of the Pyrenees’ highest northern rock face.
    
- **The Hike:** Start at _Pont d’Espagne_ (above Cauterets). It’s an easy-to-moderate walk up through pine forests and rushing streams to the stunning _Lac de Gaube_. If you want more of a challenge, continue past the lake up to the _Refuge des Oulettes de Gaube_ right under the massive Vignemale peak.
    
- **Distance:** ~8 km to the lake (longer if pushing to the refuge).
    

#### 2. Cirque de Gavarnie & Col de Tentes (From Gavarnie, France)

- **The Vibe:** A colossal natural geological amphitheater capped with hanging glaciers and one of Europe’s highest waterfalls.
    
- **The Hike:** The walk from the village of Gavarnie into the massive cirque is a classic, relatively gentle day hike. For an even higher alpine experience nearby, drive up to the _Col de Tentes_ (where the road ends at 2,200m) and hike toward the _Brèche de Roland_—a mythical gap carved into the sheer cliff border between France and Spain.
    

#### 3. Ordesa Valley / Faja de Pelay (From Torla, Spain)

- **The Vibe:** Often called the "Grand Canyon of Europe," featuring sheer vertical limestone walls and lush green beech forests.
    
- **The Hike:** Take the seasonal park bus from Torla up to the Ordesa meadow. From there, hike up the _Faja de Pelay_ ledge trail, which offers jaw-dropping cliffside views looking down into the canyon, passing the impressive Cola de Caballo waterfall on the return.

- **Cauterets or Luz-Saint-Sauveur (French Side):** Nestled right in the heart of the _Parc National des Pyrénées_, surrounded by dramatic peaks, thermal spa towns, and immediate access to legendary trails.
    
- **Torla or Aínsa (Spanish Side):** If you want to experience the dramatic limestone canyons and drier, sunnier weather of the Spanish Pyrenees, Torla sits right at the mouth of the breathtaking Ordesa National Park.

## Suggested 7-Day September Itinerary Structure

- **Day 1–2: The Drive Down**
    
    - Drive from the Netherlands through central France, stopping overnight at a scenic campsite or hotel roughly halfway (e.g., around the Auvergne volcanic region or near Limoges/Toulouse).
        
- **Days 3–6: Basecamp Cauterets or Gavarnie (French Central Pyrenees)**
    
    - Pitch your tent and spend 3 or 4 days tackling classics like **Lac de Gaube/Vignemale**, the **Cirque de Gavarnie**, and the ridge walks up from **Col de Tentes**.
        
- **Days 7–9: Cross into Torla (Spanish Pyrenees)**
    
    - Drive through the spectacular _Tunnel de Bielsa_ to the Spanish side. Base yourself near Torla and spend a couple of days hiking the **Ordesa Valley** (the _Faja de Pelay_ or _Cola de Caballo_ trails), which see brilliant early-autumn foliage changes in the beech trees later in the month.
        
- **Day 10–11: Return Drive to NL**
